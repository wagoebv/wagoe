= Access troubleshooting

Start with the user's *Rights* tab (see
xref:access-tasks.adoc#_check_what_someone_may_do[Check what someone may do]):
most answers are there. The ideas are in xref:managing-access.adoc[Managing
access].

== Forbidden (403)

The user lacks a permission. The error names it as `required-permission`.

* *No role holds it.* Find the permission under *Permissions* to see which
  roles hold it, and give one of the user's groups such a role.
* *The role is scoped and the page does not check scopes.* A scoped right
  only works where the app checks the scope; elsewhere only unscoped holders
  pass. Assign the role without a scope, or ask a developer to make the page
  check it.
* *The page has no rule.* A page the developers gave no rule admits only
  platform admins. `clojure -M:run authz-report` lists such pages.
* *The scope comes from the member and resolves to nothing.* The Rights tab
  shows *(none, so no rights)*. The app knows no value for this user, say no
  department; fix that in the app's data.

== Not found (404)

The record lies outside the user's scope. The app answers *not found* rather
than forbidden, so a user cannot learn what exists outside their part. Check
the scope on the Rights tab against the record. To widen it, assign the role
again with the other value, or without a scope.

== An empty or missing admin

*User may read no entity in the admin*:: The user holds no
`admin.<entity>/read` at all. Give a group of theirs `admin.<entity>/viewer`
for the entities they should see.

An entity is missing from the sidebar:: The sidebar and dashboard list only
what the user may read. Assign `admin.<entity>/viewer`.

They can read but not change:: They hold `viewer`, not `editor`. Buttons for
changes they may not make are not shown.

A tenant admin lost the admin:: The role Admin makes a platform admin only
without a tenant. A tenant user with that role needs groups like anyone else.

The Groups page answers Forbidden:: Users, groups, roles and permissions are
managed by users with the role Admin only. That cannot be handed out through a
group.

== Locked out

*Too many failed attempts, please try again in 15 minutes*, or a 429 from the
API: see xref:access-tasks.adoc#_locked_accounts[Locked accounts]. *Unlock* on
their *Sessions* tab ends it now.

A user who signs in with a correct password to *Invalid email or password* may
be deactivated: check *Active* on their page.

== SSO members are missing

Check the rules in
xref:access-tasks.adoc#_memberships_from_your_identity_provider[Memberships from your identity provider]
first: the names must match exactly. Then:

* *The mapping is not set up.* Without the developer's `:group-membership`
  hook, nothing comes from the provider.
* *You are looking in the wrong list.* Provider members appear on the group's
  page and the user's Rights tab, not in a permission's or role's list of
  users.
